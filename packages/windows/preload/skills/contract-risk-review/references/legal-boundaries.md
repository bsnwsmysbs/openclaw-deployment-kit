# Legal Boundaries

This skill produces draft review assistance only.

Do not:

- Declare a contract legal/illegal as a final conclusion.
- Replace licensed lawyer, in-house counsel, or approval workflow.
- Invent laws, cases, article numbers, or company rules.
- Recommend signing, rejecting, suing, or terminating as a final action.

Require human review for:

- Missing or outdated legal basis.
- Ambiguous contract facts.
- Government, state-owned asset, procurement, bidding, labor, tax, data, intellectual property, or cross-border issues.
- Large amount, long-term exclusivity, guarantee, indemnity, or unlimited liability.
