# Risk Taxonomy

Use concise tags. Multiple tags are allowed.

## Tags

- `责任不清`: Missing or conflicting responsible unit/person.
- `期限不明`: Deadline is absent or vague.
- `逾期风险`: Deadline has passed or is close with no completion proof.
- `材料不足`: Completion proof is weak or missing.
- `反馈冲突`: Different materials give inconsistent facts.
- `跨部门协同`: Completion depends on multiple units and no lead unit is clear.
- `考核影响`: May affect assessment, scoring, or accountability.
- `法律合规`: May involve legal, regulatory, contract, or audit risk.
- `资金资产`: May affect funds, assets, procurement, settlement, or payment.
- `对外报送`: May be sent to leaders, regulators, partners, or external recipients.
- `需归档`: May require official archive, signing, seal, or acceptance document.

## Human Review Questions

Write review questions as concrete missing decisions:

- `请确认该事项责任单位是否为...`
- `请补充该事项的最终完成证明或验收材料。`
- `请确认该事项是否可按已闭环处理。`
- `请确认反馈材料A与材料B不一致时以哪份为准。`
